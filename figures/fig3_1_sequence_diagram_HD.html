Figure 3.1a: Multi-agent sequence diagram for a single ordering decision cycle under active supply disruption (Day t). The five-agent architecture processes state information left-to-right through the decision pipeline. Step ③ is the critical trigger: the Environment broadcasts disruption_flag = True, which the RL Inventory Agent encodes as a state variable to order proactively (Step ⑤: 200u) before inventory breaches the classical (s, S) reorder threshold. The Governance Agent (amber zone) intercepts the RL proposal, applies the HITL threshold check, logs an SHA-256 audit entry, and approves execution to the Logistics Agent — implementing EU AI Act Article 14(4)(c) and (d) compliance-by-design. The Logistics Agent routes the shipment under a disrupted lead time of 12.5 days.
